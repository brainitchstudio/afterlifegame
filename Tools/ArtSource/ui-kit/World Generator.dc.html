How it's generated
  1. Seed. Every random choice comes from one seeded generator, so the same seed and size always rebuild the same world.
  2. Noise fields. Layered value noise gives a forest density map and scattered dirt patches.
  3. Starting base. A walled compound in the middle band of the map, gate facing the centre, with towers, starter buildings, a campfire and survivor spawns. Its tiles are marked as the buildable base zone. The wall ring is always complete, building roofs never overlap the wall, towers or each other, and nothing from the outside world is placed within a few tiles of it.
  4. Points of interest. Poisson-disc spacing, kept clear of the base. The farthest become towns; the rest are farmsteads, gas stops, ruins, chapels or camps.
  5. Roads. The base gate, every POI and a few map-edge exits are joined by a minimum spanning tree plus some loops. Each link is routed with A*, which prefers existing road and avoids forest and the base.
  6. Buildings and decor. Each POI places its buildings near its centre with the front facing a road, adds dirt yards and paths, then scatters its own decor. Chapels get a graveyard. Poles and streetlights line the roads; wrecked cars, buses and police checkpoints sit on them. Small scenes (remains, lone tents, abandoned bikes) are scattered in the open.
  7. Foliage. Dense trees where the forest field is high, scattered trees, bushes and boulders elsewhere. Mushrooms, leaf piles and fallen logs under the trees; tall grass, flowers and pebbles in the open. Roads, yards and the base clearing stay open.
  8. Zombie spawns. At every POI and along the map edge, never near the base.
